Poster as a Site of Memory: Rereading Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind Four Decades Later

It is undeniable that Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind occupies a singular position in the history of animation. Released more than forty years ago, prior even to the formal establishment of Studio Ghibli, the film already encapsulated the creative DNA of Hayao Miyazaki that would later become historically significant: ecological sensitivity, moral complexity, and a belief in coexistence rather than domination.
